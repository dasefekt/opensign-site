Questions

What owners ask us first

Who owns the website and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your business name, the hosting account is yours, and you get every login at go-live. If you ever stop working with us, everything stays with you.

What do I need to send you?

Photos (phone photos are fine; we edit them), your prices or rates, opening hours, and your WhatsApp number. We send a short checklist. If you have no photos at all, we can arrange a half-day shoot at extra cost.

What if I don't like the mockup?

You owe nothing. The mockup is ours to build and yours to reject. If you like the direction but want changes, we make them before you pay anything.

Can I still use Booking.com, Airbnb or Dineplan?

Yes, and you should. Your own site adds a channel where you keep the full price; it doesn't replace the others. For guesthouses we sync the calendar so a room booked on your site is blocked on the OTAs within minutes.

How are payments handled?

Half of the package price when you approve the mockup, half at go-live. For your customers' payments we set up a South African payment provider in your name; the money goes to your account, never through ours.

Do you work outside Cape Town?

Yes. Everything happens on WhatsApp and video calls, so we work with businesses across the Western Cape and the rest of South Africa.
